Attribution: © Open Rights Group. Source data was extracted from Blocked.org.uk, converted to hostnames, normalized, deduplicated, filtered for GL.iNet compatibility where indicated, and combined with other sources. The generated files identify source contributions in `provenance.csv`.

The historical UK ISP results are derived from Blocked.org.uk's January 2020 public export. They remain historical observations and are not presented as current blocking results.
